# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-26)

Initial release.

- Press a key or key combo to see what it's bound to, including keys bound by other addons.
- Display all modifier combos of a key at once.
- Clear a binding with one click, with undo.
- Minimap button and `/kcc`.
- Works in Retail, WoW Forever and all Classic versions.
